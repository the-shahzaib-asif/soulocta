How to use this page

Start with the first three questions if you are new: what the Destiny Matrix is, how it is calculated, and what your centre number means. Then use the rest as a reference. Every answer is short, and every answer that involves a number shows its working, because a claim you cannot check is not worth much. If your question is not here, the beginners guide is the next place to look, and the how-to guide covers the maths in full.

The maths, in one place

One rule: any number over 22 is reduced by adding its digits until it is 22 or less. Day is A, month is B, year digit sum is C, centre is the reduced sum of A, B and C. For 14 June 1995: A 14, B 6, C 6, centre 8, Justice. For 14 March 1990: A 14, B 3, C 19, centre 9, the Hermit. For 29 November 2000, this site gives centre 6, while some other sources give 3, because they define the centre as reduce(A plus B plus C plus D) instead of reduce(A plus B plus C). This site states its method openly on the how-to page, so you can compare the two and decide which one you trust. The difference matters, and hiding it would be dishonest.

How to read a chart without fooling yourself

The easiest way to fool yourself with a chart is to read it backwards: decide what you want it to say, and then find the arcana that says it. A better way is to read the centre first, write down in one sentence whether it fits, and then read the other positions and write down one surprise. The surprise is the useful part, because it is the part your expectations did not already contain. Another way to fool yourself is to read only the arcana you like. Every chart has a difficult position in it, and the difficult position is usually the one worth reading twice. If your chart has the Tower somewhere, do not skip it. Ask what in that area of your life is built on shaky ground, and what you would build instead if you were honest. A chart you only read for comfort is a mirror you only use on good hair days.

It also helps to calculate charts for people you know well, and to ask them whether their centre fits, before you decide what your own chart means. Watching someone else react to their chart teaches you how much of a reading is the chart and how much is the reader. Two people can read the same centre and hear different things, and both can be right, because a symbol is not a fact. It is a prompt. The test of a reading is not whether it sounds true in the moment. The test is whether it changes something you do on Monday morning, and whether that change makes your life a little better a month later.

What this site will not tell you

This site will not tell you whether you will get married, get a job, earn a certain amount of money, or stay healthy. It will not tell you what someone else is thinking, or what will happen next month. It will not give you a percentage for a relationship, a score for your life, or a prediction of any kind. Those are not things a birth-date chart can know, and any site that sells them is selling something else. What this site will do is show you a chart, explain every number in it, and give you words for patterns you already feel. That is a smaller promise, and it is one this site can keep.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Destiny Matrix?

It is a birth-date chart created by Natalia Ladini in 2006. It reduces day, month and year to numbers from 1 to 22, mapped to Major Arcana on an octagram.

How do you calculate a Destiny Matrix?

One rule: reduce any number over 22 by adding its digits until it is 22 or less. Day is A, month is B, year digit sum is C, and centre is the reduced sum of the three.

What is my Destiny Matrix number?

Enter your birth date in the free calculator. Your centre number is the reduced sum of your day, month and year numbers.

Is the portrait zone the centre of the chart?

No — the portrait zone is the upper half of the chart, and the centre is the middle. They are different positions, and they mean different things.

What is a karmic tail?

It is the lower part of your chart: centre, material corner and spiritual task. It describes a pattern that repeats until it is understood.

Is the Destiny Matrix the same as tarot?

No — they are different systems. They share the 22 Major Arcana, but tarot shuffles a deck to answer a question, while the Matrix reads one fixed chart from your birth date.

Is it the same as numerology?

No — they are different systems. Numerology reduces your date and name to numbers from 1 to 9, while the Matrix builds a nine-position chart of arcana from 1 to 22 using only your birth date.

Why do some sites say arcana 8 is Strength?

Some modern decks swap 8 and 11. This site follows the Tarot de Marseille, where 8 is Justice and 11 is Strength.

Do I need my birth time or birth place?

No — only your date of birth is needed. Day, month and year are enough; no birth time or place is ever asked for.

Is a Destiny Matrix chart accurate?

The calculation is accurate and checkable. The meanings are symbolic, not scientific, and no study has tested whether they predict anything.

Is it science?

No — it is not science. Treat it as entertainment and self-reflection, like tarot or numerology.

What is my personal year in 2027?

Add your day and month to 11, then reduce the total. For 14 June 1995 that is 14 plus 6 plus 11, which is 31, reducing to 4, the Emperor.
