About

A free calculator, built to be checkable.

Destiny Matrix Chart is a small, free website with one job: calculate your Destiny Matrix chart from your birth date, show every step, and never lock the result behind a payment.

The site was built because most Destiny Matrix calculators hide their method. They show a chart, then ask you to pay to read it. This site does the opposite: the full chart is free, the arithmetic is shown, and the method is stated openly, including where it differs from other schools.

How the site works

Every calculation runs in your browser, in JavaScript. Your birth date is never sent to a server, stored, or shared. There is no account, no sign-up, and no tracking of individual charts. The pages are static files, which keeps the site fast and simple.

What this site is not

This site is not a prediction service, a medical service, a financial adviser, or a legal adviser. The Destiny Matrix is a system of self-reflection created by Natalia Ladini in 2006. Its meanings are symbolic, not scientific. Read the disclaimer for the full statement.

Who built it

An independent developer in Lahore, Pakistan, who wanted a Destiny Matrix calculator that a careful reader could verify by hand. If you find a calculation error, please use the contact page and include the birth date and the number you expected. Errors will be fixed and the fix will be noted here.
