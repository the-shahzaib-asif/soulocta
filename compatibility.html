Destiny Matrix Compatibility Calculator

Enter two birth dates and get a combined Destiny Matrix compatibility chart. Each position of the shared chart is the sum of the two people's arcana at that position, reduced to 22 or less — so you can see where you match, where you differ, and where the relationship asks for work.

Compatibility here is not a score. Two people with the same centre arcana will often understand each other quickly, while two people with opposite arcana may challenge each other more. Both can work. What matters is whether you can talk about the differences honestly.

How to read this chart

Start with the combined centre. It describes the theme of the relationship itself: what you are building together, and what keeps coming up between you.

Then compare the two individual centres. If they are the same arcana, you will often want the same things. If they are different, ask what each person brings that the other lacks. The corners show shared talents, shared material life, and the shared spiritual task.

A mismatch is not a verdict. It is a conversation starter: this is where we are different, so how do we handle it?

How the combined chart is calculated

First, calculate each person's chart separately, using the same rule as the main calculator. Then add the two arcana at each position and reduce the total to 22 or less. For the example: person one centre 8, person two centre 9, so the combined centre is 8 + 9 = 17, the Star. Person one day 14 and person two day 14 give a combined day of 14 + 14 = 28, which reduces to 10, the Wheel of Fortune.

Frequently asked questions

How is Destiny Matrix compatibility calculated?

Each person's chart is calculated first, from their own birth date. Then the two arcana at each position are added together and reduced to 22 or less. For example, if one person's centre is 8 and the other's is 9, the combined centre is 17. The result is a shared chart with nine positions, just like an individual chart.

What does a matching centre arcana mean?

It usually means the two people want similar things from life and understand each other's priorities quickly. A different centre does not mean a bad match. It often means the two people bring different strengths, and the relationship works when both are respected.

Is compatibility a score or a percentage?

No, and any site that gives you a single percentage is making it up. Compatibility is a pattern across nine positions: where you match, where you differ, and where the differences are useful. Read the whole chart, not one number.

Can I calculate compatibility for a friend or family member?

Yes. The method is exactly the same for any two people: a partner, a friend, a parent, or a child. You only need their two dates of birth. The reading describes the relationship between those two people, whatever that relationship happens to be.

Is Destiny Matrix compatibility scientific?

No. It is a system of self-reflection and entertainment, created in 2006, and it has not been tested by scientific studies. Use it to start honest conversations with someone close to you, not to decide on your own whether a relationship should continue or end.
