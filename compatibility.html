How compatibility is calculated

Compatibility in the Destiny Matrix is calculated in two steps, and both steps are shown on this page so you can check them. First, each person's chart is calculated from their own birth date, using the same rule as the main calculator: day is A, month is B, year digit sum is C, and the centre is the reduced sum of A, B and C. Then the two arcana at each position are added together and reduced to 22 or less. Take the worked example on this page: 14 June 1995 gives A 14, B 6, C 6 and centre 8, Justice. 14 March 1990 gives A 14, B 3, C 19 and centre 9, the Hermit. The combined centre is 8 plus 9, which is 17, the Star. The combined A is 14 plus 14, which is 28, reduced to 10, the Wheel of Fortune. Every other position is added the same way. The result is a third chart, the combined chart, which describes the relationship itself rather than either person alone.

What the combined centre means

Some readers search for this as the matrix destiny compatibility chart, which is the same system with the words in the other order. Start with the combined centre, because it is the theme of the relationship. A combined centre of 17, the Star, suggests a relationship built on hope and honesty, where both people are recovering from something hard and trying to build something better. A combined centre of 6, the Lovers, suggests a relationship built on choice and values, where the important moments are decisions made together. A combined centre of 15, the Devil, suggests a relationship with a strong pull, where desire or habit is doing more of the work than either person admits. None of these is a verdict. A theme is a description of what the relationship asks of you, and the same theme can be lived well or badly. Two people with a difficult combined centre who talk honestly will do better than two people with an easy centre who never talk at all.

Where you match, and where you differ

After the centre, read the nine positions one by one and look for matches and differences. A match at a position means you want similar things there. A difference means you bring different strengths, and the relationship will need a conversation about that position. The most useful differences are often in the material corner and the talents, because those are the positions that decide how you handle money, work and daily life. The most useful matches are often in the centre and the day, because those decide what you value and how you act on an ordinary day. Write the matches and the differences down in two columns. Most couples are surprised by how much is in the match column, because they have been focusing on one difference for months.

Three common mistakes

The first mistake is turning compatibility into a score. Any site that gives you a single percentage for a relationship is making it up. A relationship is a pattern across nine positions, not one number, and a percentage hides the one position that actually needs work. The second mistake is using a chart to end a relationship. A chart can start a conversation, and it can name a pattern, but it cannot decide for you whether to stay or go. That decision belongs to the two people in it. The third mistake is calculating compatibility for someone without their knowledge and then treating the result as a fact about them. A chart is a tool for two people to use together, not a way of judging someone from a distance.

Compatibility and the love line

People often ask about the love line, which is the part of the chart that deals with relationships and affection. In the Destiny Matrix, love is read through the same nine positions, with extra attention to the Lovers arcana wherever it appears and to the combined centre. If the Lovers appears in either person's centre, love and choice are central to how that person lives. If it appears in the combined chart, the relationship itself is asking both people to choose, honestly, what they value. The love line is not a separate calculator on this site yet, and any site that sells you a love score from one number is selling you a shortcut that does not exist.

What a good compatibility reading looks like

A good compatibility reading is specific, not flattering. It names one position where you match well, one position where you differ, and one practical thing to try this week. If the reading could apply to any couple, it is not a reading, it is a horoscope, and you should ignore it. The combined chart on this page is specific by construction: it is calculated from two real dates, and every number in it can be checked by hand. That specificity is what makes it useful, and it is also what makes it falsifiable. If the combined centre does not feel like your relationship at all, say so, check the dates, and trust your own experience over the chart.

How to use a compatibility chart together

Calculate both charts separately first, and let each person read their own centre before you look at the combined chart. Then look at the combined centre together and ask one question: does this feel like us? If it does, ask what it asks of you both this month. If it does not, check the dates first, because a wrong date gives a wrong chart, and then talk about what the chart missed. The best use of a compatibility chart is not to prove that you match. It is to give you words for the conversation you have been avoiding, and to make that conversation easier to start.

Frequently asked questions

How is Destiny Matrix compatibility calculated?

Each person's chart is calculated first, from their own birth date. Then the two arcana at each position are added and reduced to 22 or less.

What does a matching centre arcana mean?

It usually means the two people want similar things from life. A different centre is not a bad match — it often means different strengths.

Is compatibility a score or a percentage?

No — any site giving a single percentage is making it up. Compatibility is a pattern across nine positions, not one number.

Can I calculate compatibility for a friend or family member?

Yes — the method is the same for any two people. You only need their two dates of birth.

Is Destiny Matrix compatibility scientific?

No — it is self-reflection and entertainment, not science. Use it to start honest conversations, not to end a relationship.
