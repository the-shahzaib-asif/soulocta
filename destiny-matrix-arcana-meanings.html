Which arcana is yours?

Your chart has nine positions, so nine arcana are yours in different ways. The centre is the most important: it is the theme of your whole chart, and it is the arcana to read first. After the centre, read the arcana in your money line and your love line, because those are the parts of life where the meaning becomes practical. Then read your talents, which describe what comes naturally, and your karmic tail, which describes the pattern that repeats. A single arcana is a word; nine of them together are a sentence.

What if my arcana has no full guide yet?

Then the short meaning in the 22 arcana reference is the place to start, and the calculator itself shows a short meaning for every position in your chart. Full guides are published in batches, and the table on this page is updated with each batch, so the arcana you need will appear here when its guide is ready. If you want to know when, the honest answer is that guides are written one at a time, properly, rather than generated in bulk — which is why the ones that exist are worth reading.

Is one arcana better than another?

No. Every arcana has a light side and a shadow side, and the useful question is always which side you are living right now. The Sun looks like the best card in the deck until it needs applause to feel real. The Devil looks like the worst until it tells you the honest truth about what you want. The Tower, which frightens people most, is often the card that describes a necessary ending. Read your arcana as a description, not a grade, and ask what it is describing in your life this year.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find all 22 Destiny Matrix arcana meanings?

This page lists all 22 arcana meanings in one index. It gives keywords and links to the full guides where they exist. The 22 arcana reference page gives a short meaning for every arcana, including the ones without a full guide yet.

Which arcana should I read first?

Read your centre first, because it is the theme of your whole chart. Then read the arcana in your money line and your love line, and then your talents and your karmic tail, in that order.

Why is 8 Justice and 11 Strength in the Destiny Matrix?

The Destiny Matrix follows the Tarot de Marseille numbering, where 8 is Justice and 11 is Strength. Some modern tarot decks swap those two around, which is why different sites sometimes disagree.
