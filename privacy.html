Privacy Policy

Short version: we do not collect your birth date.

What we collect

We do not collect your birth date. Every calculation on this site runs in your browser, on your own device. Your birth date is never sent to our server, never stored, never shared, and never written to a cookie or to browser storage. Your name, when you type one into a calculator, is used only to label the result on your own screen, and it is never sent anywhere either. There is no account system, no sign-up, and no database of visitors. If you close the tab, your chart is gone, and that is by design.

Cookies and advertising

This site does not set advertising cookies today, and no advertising is live yet. If advertising is added in future, it will be through Google AdSense, and this section will be updated before the first ad appears. Google and its advertising partners use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites, and to measure how those ads perform. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time at Google Ads Settings. Blocking advertising cookies will not break the calculator, because the calculator does not depend on any cookie, and this site sets no first-party tracking cookies of its own.

Your rights: GDPR and CCPA

If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you the right to know what personal data is held about you, to ask for a copy of it, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to object to its processing. Because this site collects no birth dates, no names, and no account data, there is normally nothing to copy or delete, and a request sent to hello@soulocta.me will be answered with that explanation, usually within 48 hours. If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you the right to know what personal information is collected about you, to ask for it to be deleted, and to opt out of its sale. This site does not sell personal information, and it does not collect it in the first place. If you email us, the email itself is personal information, kept only as long as needed to answer you, and never shared.

Analytics and third parties

This site runs no analytics that identifies individual visitors. The pages are hosted as static files, and the hosting provider sees the usual server logs, such as the pages requested and the time of the request, but it never receives your birth date, because the calculation never leaves your browser. Google AdSense is not live on this site yet, and no advertising cookies are set by us. If advertising or analytics are added, this page will name the provider, say exactly what it collects, and link to its opt-out.

Data retention

Because we do not collect your birth date or any account data, there is nothing to retain and nothing to delete. Emails sent to hello@soulocta.me are kept only until the conversation is finished, and then deleted. If you contact us through a social platform, that platform keeps your message under its own policy, not ours. If we ever add a contact form or an account system, this page will say exactly what is kept and for how long.

Children

This site is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before contacting us by email. The calculator itself needs no personal information, so a child can use it without giving anything away, and that is how it should stay.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the change will be made on this page, and the change will be real: the page will say what changed and why. The most likely change is the one described above, adding Google AdSense, and if that happens, this page will name it before the first ad appears. Questions about this policy belong on the contact page, or by email to hello@soulocta.me.
