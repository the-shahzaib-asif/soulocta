Privacy Policy

Short version: we do not collect your birth date.

Last updated: 14 November 2026.

What we collect

We do not collect your birth date. Every calculation runs in your browser, on your own device. It is never sent to our server, stored, or shared.

Cookies and advertising

This site may in future show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads; you can opt out at Google Ads Settings. No advertising is live yet, and no advertising cookies are set by us.

Questions about this policy

If you have a question about this policy, see the contact page for how to reach SoulOcta.

Data retention

Because we do not collect your birth date or any account data, there is nothing to retain and nothing to delete. If you contact us through a social platform, that platform keeps your message under its own policy, not ours. If we ever add a contact form or an email mailbox, this page will say so, and it will say exactly what is kept and for how long.

Third parties

The rules for using the site, including the no-warranty statement, are in the terms of use.

Two third parties are involved in this site today. GitHub hosts the pages and the public source code, and Cloudflare may host them in future; both see the usual server logs, such as the pages requested and the time of the request, and neither receives your birth date, because the calculation never leaves your browser. Google AdSense is not live on this site yet, and no advertising cookies are set by us. If advertising is added, this page will name the provider and link to its opt-out, as the cookies section above already explains.

Children

This site is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
