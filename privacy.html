Privacy Policy

Short version: we do not collect your birth date.

Last updated: 14 November 2026.

What we collect

We do not collect your birth date. Every Destiny Matrix calculation on this site runs in your browser, in JavaScript, on your own device. The date you enter is never sent to our server, stored in a database, or shared with anyone.

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information to use the calculator. A name field is offered on the calculator purely so your chart can be labelled on screen; it is not sent anywhere.

Cookies and advertising

This site may in future show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

At the time of writing, no advertising is live on this site and no advertising cookies are set by us. This policy will be updated before any advertising is enabled, and the advertising disclosure above will remain in place.

Analytics and logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as the pages requested, the time of the request, and a general location derived from your internet address, for security and for keeping the site running. Those logs are not used to identify individual visitors or to build profiles.

Children

This site is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date. If you have questions about this policy, use the contact page.
