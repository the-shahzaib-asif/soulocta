Contact

Questions, corrections, and suggestions are welcome.

Email SoulOcta at hello@soulocta.me. Every message is read, and we aim to reply within 48 hours, usually sooner. If you do not hear back in two working days, check your spam folder, then write again — messages do get lost, and a second note is never a bother.

Report a calculation error

A wrong number is the most important thing you can report, and it is the fastest thing to fix. Please include three things: the page you were on, the birth date you entered, and the number you expected versus the number you got. For example: "On the main calculator, 5 January 1990 gives centre 7, but I expected 9." That is enough to reproduce the problem in under a minute. You do not need to send a screenshot, although one helps if the chart itself looks wrong. Please do not send your full birth date by email unless you are reporting an error about that exact date — for a general question, no date is needed at all. If the error is about a shared link, paste the link itself, because the link carries only the date, and it lets us open exactly what you saw.

Suggest a guide or a feature

Suggestions are welcome, and they are how this site grows. If a guide is missing, say which arcana, karmic tail or question you want covered, and why it matters to you. If a feature is missing, say what you were trying to do when you missed it. A suggestion with a real example behind it — "I wanted to compare my chart with my sister's, and I could not find how" — is far more useful than a feature name on its own. Not every suggestion will be built, and this page will not promise that it will. The ones that get built are usually the ones that help the most people with the least complexity, because a free calculator has to stay simple to stay reliable.

What we cannot do

We cannot give personal readings by email, and we cannot give medical, financial, or legal advice. A chart is a tool for self-reflection, not a diagnosis, and an email cannot turn it into one. For personal questions, talk to a qualified professional: a doctor for health, a licensed adviser for money, a lawyer for legal questions. We also cannot calculate a chart for someone else from their name alone, because the Destiny Matrix needs a birth date, and we will not ask you for someone else's personal information. Finally, we cannot promise a reply time for suggestions, because suggestions are read in batches, while error reports are answered first.

Other ways to reach SoulOcta

The site's pictures and short guides are on Pinterest. That is the only social account SoulOcta runs. If you see another account claiming to be SoulOcta, treat it with care and do not send it personal information. How your data is handled is in the privacy policy, and the rules for using the site are in the terms of use.
