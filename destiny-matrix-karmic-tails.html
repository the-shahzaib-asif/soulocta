What a karmic tail is, and what it is not

A karmic tail is the pattern this system says repeats in your life until it is understood. Karma here means cause and effect, not punishment, and not fate. The numbers never change, because your birth date never changes, but how you live the pattern can change completely. A tail lived blindly repeats itself. A tail lived with open eyes becomes a lesson you have learned, and the lesson is the whole point of naming it.

What if my tail has no full guide yet?

Then start with the karmic tail calculator, which shows your three numbers and a short reading of them together, and read the three arcana in the 22 arcana reference — the centre, the material corner and the spiritual task, one by one. The three arcana together are most of the tail's meaning. Full guides are published in batches, and this index is updated with each batch, so your tail will appear here when its guide is ready.

Can a tail change?

The numbers cannot change, because your birth date cannot change. What changes is how you live the pattern. The same tail can be lived as a problem that repeats, or as a lesson that has been learned, and the difference is usually one honest conversation, one boundary, or one habit changed for a month. The guides on this site are built around that idea: five small, practical steps, not a grand gesture, because small steps are the ones that actually get taken.
